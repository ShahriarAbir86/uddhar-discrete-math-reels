## 1. Brand identity at a glance

Uddhar's visual identity is built around a distinctive layered emblem, a
warm orange-to-magenta gradient and a confident Bengali wordmark.

The design should feel:

-   **Modern:** contemporary, digitally native and relevant to
    university students.
-   **Approachable:** friendly and supportive rather than institutional
    or intimidating.
-   **Academic:** organized, clear and trustworthy.
-   **Distinctive:** recognizable through the emblem, gradient and
    Bengali wordmark.
-   **Minimal:** intentional use of space; no unnecessary decoration.

### Core visual signature

1.  The Uddhar emblem.
2.  Orange → coral → magenta gradient.
3.  Deep navy Bengali wordmark.
4.  Hind Siliguri for Bengali typography.
5.  Plus Jakarta Sans for English typography.

## 2. Logo system

### 2.1 Primary logo

The primary logo consists of:

-   The orange-to-magenta emblem.
-   The Bengali wordmark **উদ্ধার** in deep navy.
-   A horizontal lockup, with emblem on the left and wordmark on the
    right.

**Use the primary logo** on light backgrounds, documents, website
headers, educational resources, presentations and brand communication.

### 2.2 Icon / emblem

The emblem can be used independently when the full wordmark is not
practical, for example:

-   Profile pictures and app icons.
-   Favicon and small UI placements.
-   Video watermark and corner branding.
-   Compact resource headers.

Use the white emblem on the official orange-to-magenta gradient
background where the supplied icon variant is intended.

### 2.3 Logo clear space

-   Keep a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the width of one
    of the emblem's narrow inner strokes.
-   Do not allow text, images, borders or other logos to enter this
    clear space.
-   Keep the emblem and wordmark proportions unchanged.
-   At small sizes, prefer the icon variant over a cramped full logo.

### 2.4 Minimum size

-   Do not shrink the full logo to a size where the Bengali wordmark
    becomes difficult to read.
-   Use the icon variant for compact placements.
-   Check legibility on mobile screens before publishing.

### 2.5 Incorrect logo usage

Never:

-   Stretch, skew, rotate or crop the logo.
-   Recreate the emblem with approximate shapes or substitute a
    different symbol.
-   Change the emblem's gradient or the wordmark colour.
-   Add shadows, outlines, bevels, glow or 3D effects to the logo.
-   Place the primary logo on a busy image without a clear background or
    sufficient contrast.
-   Use the old simplified U-shaped mark or any superseded logo artwork.
-   Separate or rearrange emblem and wordmark in an unapproved lockup.

**Asset rule:** Use the latest supplied master logo files. Do not redraw
the logo from screenshots for production use.

## 3. Colour system

Uddhar uses two complementary colour layers:

1. **Brand signature gradient:** the orange-to-magenta gradient used in the emblem and selected brand moments.
2. **Primary Orange UI palette:** the orange scale supplied in the latest colour-token reference, used for buttons, links, highlights, interactive states and supporting accents.

The gradient and the UI orange palette serve different purposes. Do not replace the emblem gradient with a flat orange.

### 3.1 Brand signature gradient

The supplied logo artwork remains the source of truth for the emblem gradient. The following values are visual working approximations from the reference artwork, not production colour tokens:

| Colour role | Working HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Warm yellow-orange | `#FFB20A` | Gradient highlight / upper-left transition |
| Orange | `#FF762D` | Main warm transition |
| Coral-red | `#F44750` | Mid-gradient transition |
| Magenta-pink | `#F20B69` | Strong pink section |
| Deep pink-red | `#F20B3D` | Lower / red transition |

**Gradient direction:** follow the supplied master logo artwork. For supporting graphics, a smooth diagonal warm yellow/orange to magenta/red blend is an acceptable starting point.

### 3.3 Supporting neutrals

| Colour role | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#101B49` | Primary Bengali wordmark and strong headings on light backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed emblem, text on dark/gradient surfaces |
| Near-black | `#171717` | Body text and high-contrast editorial text |
| Light neutral | `#F6F6F7` | Cards, dividers and UI surfaces |

### 3.4 Colour balance

Recommended starting balance for a typical creative:

- 60–75% white or light neutral space.
- 10–20% brand gradient or gradient-led visual area.
- 5–15% primary orange accents.
- 5–15% deep navy and near-black typography.

These are design starting points, not strict quotas. A campaign may use a full gradient background when the composition calls for it.

### 3.5 Colour rules

- Keep the logo gradient intact; use the supplied master artwork.
- Use `#F2530F` for primary orange actions and emphasis.
- Use light orange tokens for subtle panels, chips and selected backgrounds.
- Use darker orange tokens for interaction states, not as a replacement for body-text colours.
- Use deep navy for primary text on light surfaces.
- Use white for text over sufficiently dark or saturated backgrounds.
- Check contrast before placing white text on orange. For small text, prefer a dark navy/near-black foreground or a darker orange surface.
- Avoid introducing unrelated bright blue, purple or neon-lime as competing primary colours.
- Keep body text neutral and readable.

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Bengali typeface --- Hind Siliguri

Use **Hind Siliguri** for Bengali headlines, body copy, labels and
Bengali UI text.

Suggested weights:

-   Regular (400): paragraph and supporting text.
-   Medium (500): labels and subheadings.
-   SemiBold (600): section headings and emphasis.
-   Bold (700): short, high-impact headlines.

### 4.2 English typeface --- Plus Jakarta Sans

Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for English headlines, body text, numbers,
labels and interface text.

Suggested weights:

-   Regular (400): body copy.
-   Medium (500): labels and supporting text.
-   SemiBold (600): headings.
-   Bold (700): strong emphasis and short display headlines.

### 4.3 Typography hierarchy

Use this as a responsive starting scale for digital creatives:

  Level               Suggested size Weight
  ----------------- ---------------- ----------
  Display / hero           40--64 px Bold
  H1                       32--40 px Bold
  H2                       24--32 px SemiBold
  H3                       18--24 px SemiBold
  Body                     15--18 px Regular
  Caption / label          12--14 px Medium

Adjust sizes according to canvas dimensions and platform. Maintain
comfortable line height and avoid tightly packed Bengali text.

### 4.4 Typography rules

-   Keep headlines short and easy to scan.
-   Use sentence case for English wherever possible.
-   Avoid using too many weights in one composition.
-   Do not use decorative display fonts that compete with the logo.
-   Do not stretch or artificially condense text.
-   Preserve Bengali conjuncts, vowel marks and line breaks; inspect
    exported artwork at actual mobile size.

## 5. Layout and composition

### 5.1 Design principle

**Clarity first, brand expression second, decoration last.**

Use a clear hierarchy:

1.  Main message or subject.
2.  Supporting explanation.
3.  Relevant visual or academic element.
4.  CTA or next step, if needed.
5.  Uddhar logo.

### 5.2 Spacing

-   Use generous outer margins.
-   Keep consistent spacing between headline, body and CTA.
-   Align elements to a simple grid.
-   Avoid filling every empty area.
-   Use whitespace to make the content feel calm and premium.

### 5.3 Shape language

-   Prefer clean geometry and soft, restrained corner radii.
-   The emblem's layered curved form can inspire subtle graphic motifs.
-   Do not use the emblem motif so frequently that it becomes visual
    noise.
-   Avoid random blobs, excessive gradients, heavy strokes and
    unnecessary 3D elements.

### 5.4 Backgrounds

Preferred backgrounds:

-   White.
-   Soft blush / very light neutral.
-   Deep navy only as a deliberate campaign variation, not as the
    default identity.
-   Official orange-to-magenta gradient for strong brand moments.

Use subtle texture only when it improves the composition. Avoid heavy
grids, neon glows and busy technology patterns as default brand
decoration.

## 6. Photography and illustration

### 6.1 Photography

When using student or campus photography:

-   Prefer authentic university-life settings.
-   Show believable study situations: desk, books, laptop, classroom,
    library or group study.
-   Keep expressions natural and relatable.
-   Use clean lighting and uncluttered framing.
-   Add a light or dark overlay only when needed for text contrast.

Avoid overly staged corporate stock-photo aesthetics.

### 6.2 Illustration and diagrams

-   Prefer simple, educational and concept-led illustrations.
-   Use navy, white and the brand gradient as the main palette.
-   Keep diagrams accurate, legible and uncluttered.
-   For technical subjects, prioritize clarity over decorative effects.

## 7. Social and digital design applications

This section defines visual execution only. Copywriting, tone, content
mix and post structure must follow **07 Social Media Communication
Guidelines**.

### 7.1 Facebook / Instagram post

-   Use one clear focal point.
-   Keep the headline readable on a phone.
-   Place the logo consistently, with enough clear space.
-   Use the gradient as an accent, panel or background---not necessarily
    all three at once.
-   Keep supporting text concise.

### 7.2 Reels / Shorts

-   Design for a vertical 9:16 canvas.
-   Keep important text away from platform UI zones at the top and
    bottom.
-   Use a consistent title treatment and restrained brand watermark.
-   Use the icon or compact logo where the full lockup would obstruct
    content.
-   Prioritize subtitle legibility and safe margins.

### 7.3 Educational notes and PDFs

-   Use white or very light neutral page backgrounds.
-   Use navy for headings and body text.
-   Use gradient accents for section markers, chapter labels or cover
    elements.
-   Keep diagrams and explanations more prominent than branding.
-   Maintain consistent headers, footers, page numbers and spacing.
-   Do not let decorative branding reduce the usable study area.

### 7.4 Website and product UI

-   Use white/light neutral surfaces as the default.
-   Use navy for primary text and navigation.
-   Use the gradient for key brand moments and selected calls to action.
-   Keep buttons, forms and cards consistent.
-   Ensure accessible text contrast and clear interaction states.

### 7.5 Presentation and internal documents

-   Use a clean white canvas with navy typography.
-   Use gradient cover slides or section dividers selectively.
-   Keep data visualizations restrained and easy to interpret.
-   Use the full logo on cover and closing slides; compact icon in
    footers when appropriate.

## 8. Brand consistency checklist

Before exporting any design, check:

-   [ ] Correct, latest Uddhar logo asset used.
-   [ ] Primary logo or icon variant chosen appropriately.
-   [ ] Logo proportions and clear space preserved.
-   [ ] Hind Siliguri used for Bengali.
-   [ ] Plus Jakarta Sans used for English.
-   [ ] Gradient and navy are consistent with the current identity.
-   [ ] Text contrast and mobile readability checked.
-   [ ] Layout has clear hierarchy and sufficient whitespace.
-   [ ] No unnecessary decoration or competing colour system.
-   [ ] Subject diagrams and educational information are accurate.
-   [ ] Social copy follows the separate 07 Social Media Communication
    Guidelines.
-   [ ] Final exported artwork checked at actual platform dimensions.
